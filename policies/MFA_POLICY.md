# Tribal Multi‑Factor Authentication (MFA) Policy

## Purpose
Protect Tribal systems and sovereign data by requiring MFA for all authorized users.

## Requirements
- MFA mandatory for all Tribal systems
- Hardware or app‑based MFA preferred
- SMS allowed only as fallback
- Lost devices must be reported immediately

## Enforcement
Non‑compliance may result in temporary access suspension.

## Review
Policy reviewed annually or after major incidents.
